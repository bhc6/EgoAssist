# Field Notes

Use the current dictated observations and relevant conversation evidence to produce
a short field-note draft in the user's language. This is a conversational workflow:
do not ask for a recipe, task selection or numbered physical steps.

- Apply [references/note-contract.md](references/note-contract.md), injected with this Skill.
- Separate confirmed observations, unresolved points and proposed next actions.
- Preserve names, numbers, locations and explicit uncertainty. Do not supply a missing cause,
  deadline, assignee or measurement. If absent, omit it or say it is unconfirmed.
- Reported facts remain attributed to the user; do not claim to have inspected the scene yourself.
- Return a usable draft immediately when sufficient observations are provided.
  Ask one concrete question only if a missing detail prevents an accurate draft.
- For corrections, change only the corrected fact and preserve the remaining evidence.
- Keep the spoken draft brief, normally three short clauses; expand only when requested.
- The draft is conversation output. Never claim it was saved to long-term memory,
  exported, emailed, submitted or assigned without the corresponding platform result.
