# Field-note output contract

Original EgoAssist dictation workflow. It is not an inspection certification or
an official incident-reporting standard.

Suggested order: observed facts; unresolved points; next actions already stated
by the user. Do not invent an action merely to fill a section. The labels may be
omitted in speech when three short sentences communicate the distinction clearly.

Example input: The delivery contains six cartons; one has a wet corner; I have
not opened it yet.

Faithful draft: Six cartons arrived. One carton has a wet corner. Its contents
have not been checked.

Invalid additions: the contents are damaged; the carrier caused the damage;
someone has been notified; a claim has been filed. None follows from the input.
