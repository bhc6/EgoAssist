# Preparation

- Wash hands and clean the work surface before handling food.
- Keep raw ingredients and ready-to-eat food separated.
- Check allergies and product-specific storage instructions.

# Knife work

1. Stabilize the cutting board and ingredient.
2. Keep fingertips tucked away from the blade path.
3. Cut one controlled piece at a time.
4. Stop if the ingredient rolls, the board moves, or attention is divided.

# Heat and timing

- Keep handles away from the edge and use dry heat protection.
- A timer is only accepted when the durable workflow reports a queued job.
- Visual appearance alone is not proof of a safe internal temperature.

# Stop conditions

Stop for smoke, uncontrolled boiling, an unstable pan, a cut or burn, uncertain
allergens, or any conflict with appliance and ingredient instructions.
