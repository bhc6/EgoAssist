# Assembly evidence policy

Load this policy for every assembly task.

- Treat the exact product manual and visible part labels as primary evidence.
- Treat bundled workflows as demonstrations, not replacements for a manufacturer manual.
- Ask for the model number or a clear manual image when dimensions, torque, orientation, or rated load are product-specific.
- Stop before irreversible or load-bearing actions when evidence conflicts or is missing.

Research grounding: HoloAssist models mistake, intervention, and verification in real procedural tasks; the bundled local reference is under `research_references/HoloAssist` when research assets are present. The Skill does not depend on that repository at runtime.
