# Before installation

- Match the cartridge model and housing against the manufacturer manual.
- Inspect the cartridge, gasket, housing groove, and contact surfaces for damage.
- Stop if a product-specific instruction conflicts with this generic reference.

# Preparation and gasket

1. Rinse or soak the cartridge only as its manufacturer requires.
2. Clean the gasket groove and place the correct gasket flat in the groove.
3. Do not stretch, twist, stack, or substitute a seal.
4. Keep sealing surfaces free of grit and lubricant unless the manual permits it.

# Cartridge insertion

1. Align the cartridge using its printed arrow, key, tab, or locating mark.
2. Insert it vertically without forcing or cross-threading the housing.
3. Stop if alignment marks do not meet or resistance differs from the manual.

# Verification

- Reassemble and flush for the duration specified by the manufacturer.
- Inspect every seal while the system is pressurized.
- Stop use for leakage, a displaced gasket, unusual pressure, damaged threads,
  or water quality that does not meet the manufacturer acceptance criteria.
